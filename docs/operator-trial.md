The package organizes supplied evidence and judgments; it does not discover the goal, authenticate a reviewer or turn check counts into general correctness. Neither is an independent human assessment.

## Decision

Retain this path when another reviewer needs a durable selected handoff with original evidence and version-bound decisions. It still asks the operator to choose inputs, inspect evidence, record judgment, create a fresh reviewed share and arrange trusted serving. An existing PR/CI workflow is an appropriate simpler choice when that durable export is unnecessary. These two cases establish a working repeatable path, not a productivity gain or broad team adoption.

Further runtime simplification should target observed recurring operator work. This trial does not justify a new GUI, workflow framework or broad rewrite. Independent human usability and time savings remain unmeasured.
